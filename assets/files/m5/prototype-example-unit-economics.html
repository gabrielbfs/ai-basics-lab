Trail Club unit economics

How to use this: change any input and the results update. Numbers start from the example assumptions table (fictional company).

Gross profit per member per month
–
Price × gross margin.
Lifetime value (LTV)
–
Gross profit per month ÷ monthly churn.
LTV / CAC
–
How many times a member pays back what it cost to win them. 3 or more is a common target.
Months to pay back CAC
–
CAC ÷ gross profit per month.
LTV/CAC is below 3: each new member doesn't earn back enough of what it cost to win them.
